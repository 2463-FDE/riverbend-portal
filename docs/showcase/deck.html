The one to remember

This is one person.

ChartName on fileDate of birthAllergies

1330Maria Gonzales1971-03-02penicillin
1588M. Gonzalez1971-02-03none recorded

Same address, same phone, same social security number. Three records, because self-service intake has no way to recognise a returning patient. The middle row is the only one carrying her penicillin allergy — and it is not the record her portal login is attached to.

Before this work, a clinician opening her chart could see “no allergies recorded” and be looking at a complete-looking record that was missing the one thing that could hurt her.

The system now resolves the three into one person, and says so on screen. You will see this in the demo.

Why it went unnoticed

Both of these are true at the same time.

1.00 Search accuracy, against the previous vendor’s own test set
0.56 How much of the actual patient the search was looking at

The search finds everything it was given. It was given a third of the patient. A test written over already-split records cannot notice that the records are split — so the score was high, and correct, and measuring the wrong question.

What changedThere are two scorecards now. One for whether the search works, one for whether the record is whole. Staff can see both, without asking us.

Safety

The assistant works. What matters is what it refuses to do.

Accepted

“Arrive fifteen minutes early, bring your insurance card and photo ID, and do not eat for eight hours before your blood draw.”

supported by the source · shown to patient

Withheld

The same summary, plus a medication instruction that was never in the visit notes.

clinical claim not in source · held for review

A summary that is 95% right and invents one medication is more dangerous than an obviously wrong one, because it reads as competent. The system checks for clinical claims that are not in the source text, and any hit withholds the answer rather than softening it.

Access

Who can see what, and who decides.

ThenNow
Any login could open any patient’s chart by changing a number in the address bar The check runs before anything is fetched. An unauthorised chart and a non-existent one return the same answer, so nobody can probe for real patient IDs
No record of who read what Assembling a record across several charts pauses for a named person to release it

The release decision cannot be made by the patient it concerns, and cannot be made by the staff member who requested it. Two people, or it does not happen.

The AI never widens access. It receives what a person is already allowed to see, and assembles within it.

Sign the AWS agreement

The business associate agreement covering AI services. Until it is signed, this runs on test data only.

Decide on data retention
